What I hear from almost every first-time buyer

Here in Utah County, most first-time buyers think they're further from owning than they really are. It usually comes down to one of these three beliefs:

I need 20% down.

A lot of buyers qualify for low-down-payment loans, and they never find out because they assume 20% is the rule.

My credit has to be perfect.

Perfect credit isn't the bar people think it is. What matters is where your credit actually is and which options fit it. That's something we can check together.

I need a big savings account first.

Many first-time buyers qualify for assistance programs they've never heard of. You can't use a program you don't know exists.

Why I work with first-time buyers
